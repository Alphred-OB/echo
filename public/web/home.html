How does Echo sign me in without typing passwords?
Echo plays a single-use sound signal from your computer speaker. Your personal phone listens through its microphone, verifies the identity code, and signs you in securely with a single tap.
What if my phone battery dies or I don't have it nearby?
You can always fall back to a one-time magic sign-in link sent directly to your registered email address. It expires after 15 minutes and can only be used once.
Can someone record the sound and play it back to log in?
No. Every sound code is uniquely generated for that single login attempt, automatically expires after 30 seconds, and is deleted from the server the instant it is used.
Do I need to install any app or browser extension?
No installation is required! Echo runs natively inside standard modern web browsers on your computer and phone using built-in web audio and security APIs.
Is the sound signal audible or disruptive to people around me?
By default, Echo uses near-ultrasonic frequencies above 17 kHz that human ears cannot hear. If your device speaker prefers audible frequencies, you can also toggle audible mode anytime.
